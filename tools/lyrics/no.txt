# VOICE words (JIANT 0.8.5): the Norwegian / Old Norse word bank of the lyric generator (tools/gen_lyrics.py ->
# felucca_lyrics.h). Plain ASCII, no accents (the device's font and keys have none): ae oe aa for ae, o-slash and
# a-ring, dh th for eth and thorn, kj sj skj rs for their sounds, y for the front-rounded vowel. Norwegian is read by
# rules on the device (sing.c sy_no_tok, tools/gen_lyrics.py no_tokens), so any respelled Norwegian text sings; these
# words are only what the dice picks from. Curated single words only: no lyric lines or fragments.
# A line: a theme (LOVE NIGHT SEA SKY ROAD DREAM FIRE) or FN (the small words of every theme), a category, words.
#   N nouns (/f feminine), V verbs, A adjectives, D determiners, P prepositions, C conjunctions.
